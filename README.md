## 🕹️ About

Star Trek: Outposts Unknown is a narrative-driven outpost-building strategy game set in the 23rd century, alongside Star Trek: Strange New Worlds. Lead the crew of the U.S.S. Resonant through the X’Lehari System, researching strange worlds, building facilities, and confronting a mysterious cosmic force. This raw Windows pre-release build surfaced ahead of schedule and remains unfinished.

For Star Trek fans and strategy players who enjoy deliberate colony building, exploration, research, and story-driven sci-fi with an unfinished edge.

> ⚠️ **This is an unofficial leaked pre-release build.** The game is under active development: expect bugs, unfinished content, and balance changes.Archive is password protected (`2026`) to compatibility automated DMCA bots and hosting service scanners that would otherwise delete the files within hours.

## ✨ Features

- 🪐 **Unknown Worlds** — Explore hostile planets, abandoned ships, ancient ruins, and unexplored frontier locations.
- 🏗️ **Outpost Construction** — Build and expand complex research facilities across the X’Lehari System.
- 🔬 **Research Operations** — Study anomalies, gather resources, and develop your outposts through scientific work.
- 👩‍🚀 **Crew Management** — Direct your crew aboard the U.S.S. Resonant and manage their work across the frontier.
- 📖 **Narrative Campaign** — Uncover a story about a mysterious cosmic threat endangering a new ally.
- ⚠️ **Frontier Decisions** — Guide your mission through dangerous encounters and an unstable developing civilization.
